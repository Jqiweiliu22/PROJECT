# English and Noongar interface notes

The site uses English for its complete functional interface and a small set of individually sourced Noongar labels. It is not a complete Noongar translation. Catalogue titles remain in the original English supplied by NMA or SLWA, and no cultural narratives have been generated or translated.

Noongar is used here for the southwest Western Australian language context. The collection sample is Australia-wide; this interface choice does not identify an object's language or Country. A complete translated interface should be developed with an appropriate Noongar language organisation or speaker. No endorsement or community review is claimed.

These are individual published words or question forms, not newly composed Noongar sentences. Source spellings are preserved; no claim of one universal Noongar spelling or dialect is made. The record-search algorithm searches English catalogue fields, not Noongar translations.
